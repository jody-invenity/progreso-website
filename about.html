About

Enterprise-level insight for South Africa's small businesses.

Progreso Solutions is an owner-led consultancy based in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, working with SME owners across South Africa, in person and remotely.

Founder, Progreso Solutions

Hi, I'm Jody Blake.

I'm dedicated to bringing enterprise-level insight to South Africa's small business community. I founded Progreso Solutions in 2024 to partner with SME owners on systematic improvement: diagnosing the operational bottlenecks holding the business back, designing cost-saving strategies, and building plans for sustained profit.

I oversee the implementation too: efficient operational workflows and sales processes that keep increasing the value of every customer. That foundation of continuous improvement is how clients beat the high local failure rate for small businesses.

Our first conversation is about your vision: why you started, who you help and where you want to go. We'll dig into the problems you're facing, or the ones you suspect are lurking beneath the surface. No fees, no pressure. Just a conversation between two people who care about building something that lasts.
